# Cubism rig checklist

1. Importar PSD validado.
2. Criar ArtMeshes.
3. Organizar warp/rotation deformers.
4. Rig de cabeça X/Y/Z.
5. Eyes open e eye ball X/Y.
6. Brows.
7. Mouth open/form com interior completo.
8. Body angles e shoulders.
9. Breath discreto.
10. Physics: bangs, side hair, tips e identity strand.
11. Expressions: neutral, happy, curious, focused, concerned, amused, tired, surprised.
12. Motions: idle_soft, attention, head_tilt, nod, thinking, alert.
13. Texture atlas.
14. Exportar model3/moc3/physics/textures/expressions/motions e executar `scripts/validate-live2d-export.ps1`.
